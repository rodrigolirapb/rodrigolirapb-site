# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Agency hiring managers (Group AD, VP/Head of Client Services), agency recruiters, agency founders hiring a contractor, and peers/referrers who might share the site. They land here to decide, in about 30 seconds, whether Rodrigo Borges can run and grow a book of business for a Toronto ad agency, right now, with an AI-savvy edge.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that repositions Rodrigo as an account director for the AI era and convinces a hiring manager to book a call. It functions like a well-run agency's case-study page: a one-page "brief" about him on Home, industry stories as brief cards, a living newsletter feed, and signals that he's current (recent posts, recently read books, an up-to-date Now page).

## Brand Personality

Confident, specific, first-person, never salesy. Three words: sharp, current, substantive. Feels like a strategist's brief, not a resume and not a developer portfolio.

## Anti-references

- Not a developer portfolio (no code-flex, no terminal/monospace aesthetic).
- Not a generic "modern SaaS" template (no gradient-text hero metrics, no stock photography).
- Never draws attention to time away from agencies; no "outsider" or "every seat at the table" framing.

## Design Principles

- The brief is the signature device: labelled fields, scannable structure, proof over adjectives.
- One memorable moment per page beats effects everywhere; motion must never delay reading.
- Numbers and results are never invented; only the proof points already on file are used.
- Fast and legible on a phone first; desktop flourishes layer on top, not the reverse.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus must stay visible, contrast must stay strong, and all motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No client names anywhere. Full email address never appears in page HTML.
