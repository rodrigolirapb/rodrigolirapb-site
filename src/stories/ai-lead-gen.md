problem: "A new product aimed at North American electric utilities and cooperatives needed a partner route to market, and no lead list to start from."
moves: "Set up the target structure first, a Notion table with the fields that mattered, then directed an AI agent working in the browser to run a Hunter.io search, classify each company by fit and verify it, correcting the agent when it missed results on a scrolling page or chased the wrong keywords."
result: "128 leads labelled by fit (strong, low, not a fit, unverified), ready for prioritization, plus a short list of industry networks worth joining for visibility."
ai: "The agent did the searching, cross-checking and classifying. Explaining the business logic behind a good fit, reviewing its output and refining the search terms stayed a human job throughout."
