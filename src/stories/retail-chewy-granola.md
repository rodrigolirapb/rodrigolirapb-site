problem: "An interactive material study, a jelly you could poke, wobble and slice in the browser, made me want one of my own, built around the snack always on my desk: a chewy granola bar."
moves: "Directed Claude Code to build Chewy Granola, starting from a public project and tweaking it: a hand that grabs, bends and twists the bar; a knife that comes down, squashes it, then cuts clean; a snap action that breaks it like your hands would, crumbs included, with the mass reading dropping as they fall; and a jelly mode that turns the same bar into honey jelly that wobbles and springs back. To get the surface right, I photographed a real MadeGood bar and we rebuilt it from that photo: whole oats, glossy syrup, chocolate drops almost a centimetre wide."
result: "A live, playable material study, shipped by dragging the files onto Vercel."
ai: "One real photo did more for the surface than ten rounds of describing it, and specific feedback, like 'the knife should work like the snap,' moved it further than generic notes ever did."
